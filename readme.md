# Skill Verify

## The Problem
A degree or certificate doesn't prove someone can actually do the job — and a lack of one doesn't prove they can't. Formal education often bundles in requirements irrelevant to the actual skill (why should a penetration tester need physics?), while the job market — especially in Nigeria — is shaped heavily by credentials, connections, and nepotism rather than verified ability. Skill Verify exists to separate the two: what you can *actually do*, tested directly, independent of who you know or where you studied.

## What It Does
Pick a field, take a 10-question test, get scored instantly:
- **80%+** → Pass, with a certificate
- **Below 80%** → A breakdown of the areas you got wrong, so you know exactly what to study next

No accounts, no setup — just proof of competence, on demand.

Currently live for two fields:
- Cybersecurity Basics
- Web Development Basics

## Tech Stack
Built intentionally minimal — plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, with all scoring done client-side. No backend, no database, no framework. This keeps it fast, dependency-free, and fully hosted on GitHub Pages — proof that a working idea doesn't need a heavy stack to demonstrate value.

## Roadmap
- Expert-drafted, larger question banks per field
- User accounts to save progress and track improvement over time
- Timed questions, to raise the cost of looking up answers mid-test
- AI-detection check on free-text answers, to flag likely non-original responses
- Partnerships with companies to recognize Skill Verify certification directly
